We make these for brands.

The characters on this site are programs, not footage. That makes a new 30-second film fast to produce, easy to change, and consistent from the first episode to the fiftieth.

Email us about a filmSee the characters

What you get

A vertical film of about 30 seconds, ready to post on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and your own site: script, animation, voice or no voice, captions and sound. We can build a character for your brand, or explain your product with one clear idea per film.

How it runs

  1. You tell us the one thing the viewer should remember.
  2. We write the script and check every claim in it.
  3. We build and animate it in code, then add voice, captions and sound.
  4. You get the finished file. Changes are edits to the script, not a reshoot.
