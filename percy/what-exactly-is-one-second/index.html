What exactly is one second?

The short answer

Since 1967 a second is not a slice of a day. It is 9,192,631,770 ticks of a caesium atom. Percy's whole 30-second life is about 276 billion of them.

What we checked

Second defined in 1967 as 9,192,631,770 caesium periods; before that, a fraction of the day; 30 s ≈ 276 billion ticks.

Confirmed (arithmetic checked) on 26 Nov 2026, against the source below.

Sources and notes

  • BIPM, Resolution 1 of the 13th General Conference on Weights and Measures (1967). bipm.org
