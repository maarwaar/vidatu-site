Vidatu / About

Hidden rules, made visible.

Vidatu makes 30-second films about one true, surprising thing at a time. An alien audits human habits. A progress bar gets one life per fact. A hydrogen atom remembers the early universe.

Made in code

Nothing here is filmed, and no AI video generator is used. Each character is a small program: shapes, materials, lights and a list of timed lines. A computer draws the film one frame at a time. Because the characters are programs, the same ones run live on this site, where you can move them and tap them.

The voices

The character voices are AI-generated and are labelled that way on every film. The Lab films have no voice at all: captions, and sound that is also made in code.

The facts

Every number in a film has a named source, listed on the film's page and on the sources page. Scripts are researched and written by the SellnShip team with the help of AI tools, every claim is checked against its source, and nothing is released until a person has watched and listened to it.

Credits

Vidatu is created and produced by SellnShip Solutions Pvt. Ltd., Chandigarh, India. The idea, the seven characters, the scripts, the fact-checking and the code that draws every frame are SellnShip's work. The same team makes films like these for brands.
