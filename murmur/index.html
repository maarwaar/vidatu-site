Episodes

  1. Chapter 001

    One of these 8,300 dots is you

    Murmur is made of 8,300 dots, one for every million people alive. About six in ten live in Asia and nearly two in ten in Africa.

  2. Chapter 002

    Four people are born every second

    Every second about four people are born and two die. The world grows by roughly 190,000 people a day.

  3. Chapter 003

    You are part of the newest 7%

    About 117 billion people have ever been born. Everyone alive today is roughly 7% of the human story.
