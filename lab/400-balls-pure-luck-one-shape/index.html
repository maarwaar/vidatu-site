400 balls, pure luck, one shape

The short answer

Every peg is a coin flip: left or right. Nobody can say where one ball lands. But drop 400 and the pile takes the same shape every time: the bell curve.

Sources and notes

  • A simulation of the Galton board (Francis Galton, 1889), with a fair coin flip at each of 12 rows of pegs.
