Sources and corrections

Every claim in every film, with where it came from. All of them were last checked on 26 Nov 2026.

How we check

A script is not animated until each number in it has a named source: a published study, a space agency, a standards body or a national statistics office. Where sources disagree, we use the cautious figure and say so. Simulations are labelled as simulations.

Corrections

One so far. In "Would you move to Mars?" the average temperature was first given as minus 63 °C. Agencies publish values from minus 53 to minus 63, so the line now says "about minus sixty". Corrected before release, on 26 Nov 2026.
