Could you outrun a T. rex?

The short answer

Yup says easy. Nope has numbers: a 2017 computer model found that T. rex could not run without breaking its legs, but its fast walk still reached about 19 km/h (12 mph). You decide.

What we checked

A 2017 computer model found running would overload the leg bones of T. rex, limiting it to walking.

Confirmed on 27 Nov 2026, against the sources below.

Sources and notes

  • Sellers et al. (2017), "Investigating the running abilities of Tyrannosaurus rex using stress-constrained multibody dynamic analysis", PeerJ 5: e3420. doi.org
  • The paper limits T. rex to walking. The figure of about 19 km/h (12 mph) is as reported from the study; the paper itself gives no single top speed for that walk.
