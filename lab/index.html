Experiments

  1. Experiment 001

    15 pendulums, one hidden rule

    Fifteen pendulums, each a little shorter than the last. In 24 seconds the longest makes 18 swings, the next 19, and so on up to 32.

  2. Experiment 002

    400 balls, pure luck, one shape

    Every peg is a coin flip: left or right. Nobody can say where one ball lands.

  3. Experiment 003

    40 pendulums, almost the same start

    Forty double pendulums start a billionth of a degree apart. For about eleven seconds they move as one.
