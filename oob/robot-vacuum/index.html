Why do people name their robot vacuums?

The short answer

Because a machine that moves around the home by itself gets treated like a member of the household. In a 2007 Georgia Tech study of 30 owners, 21 had given their robot vacuum a name and 16 referred to it as "he".

Humans built a flat, round machine to vacuum the floor. It has no face and no voice. It bumps into the furniture and turns around.

Researchers at Georgia Tech visited 30 households that owned one. Most had named it, and more than half spoke of it as "he". Some owners tidied the floor before switching it on, to make its job easier. One introduced it to his parents.

The researchers called it an intimate home appliance: owners related to it less like a tool and more like part of the household.

Questions people ask

Is it normal to name a robot vacuum?

In the 2007 study it was the usual thing to do: 21 of the 30 owners had named theirs.

Why do people call a robot vacuum "he"?

The study does not settle why. It records that 16 of the 30 owners spoke of it as "he", along with other ways of treating it like a person or a pet.
