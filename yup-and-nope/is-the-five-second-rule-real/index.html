Is the five-second rule real?

The short answer

Bacteria can transfer in under a second. But dry food picks up far less, and carpet passes on less than tile. So who wins?

What we checked

Bacteria can transfer in under a second; moisture matters; carpet transfers less than tile.

Confirmed on 26 Nov 2026, against the source below.
