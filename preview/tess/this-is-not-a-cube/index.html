This is not a cube

The short answer

Tess is four-dimensional, so what you see is only her shadow. A flat creature watching a ball pass through its world sees a dot, a circle, then nothing. You see her the same way.

Sources and notes

  • Idea from Edwin Abbott's "Flatland" (1884).
