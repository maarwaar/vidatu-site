  1. Old H

    The day the universe became see-through

    For about 380,000 years the universe was a hot fog that light could not cross. Then atoms formed, the fog cleared, and that first light is still arriving today.

  2. Old H

    How a star makes sunshine

    Deep inside the Sun it is about 15 million degrees. Hydrogen nuclei fuse into helium, a little mass goes missing, and it leaves as light.

  3. Old H

    Your glass of water is 13.8 billion years old

    Almost every hydrogen atom in your water was made in the first minutes after the Big Bang. It has been a cloud, a comet and an ocean before it reached your glass.

  4. Gulp

    What if a tiny black hole ate the Moon?

    Gulp is a polite, pocket-sized black hole. Without the Moon, tides would shrink to about a third, nights would be far darker, and Earth's tilt would slowly become unstable.

  5. Gulp

    A grapefruit-sized black hole in your kitchen

    A black hole the size of a grapefruit would weigh about as much as seven Earths. Your kitchen would not survive the visit.

  6. Yup & Nope

    Would you move to Mars?

    About minus 60 degrees on average, air that is 95% carbon dioxide, and a day only 37 minutes longer than ours. But you would weigh about a third as much.
