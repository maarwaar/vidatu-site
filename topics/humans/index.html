    Why loading bars freeze at 99%

    Percy is a progress bar with 30 seconds to live. Many bars count finished tasks, not time, and the last task is often the slowest.

  4. Percy

    The food that never goes off

    Sealed honey barely spoils: almost no water, high acidity, and an enzyme from bees that makes hydrogen peroxide. Honey thousands of years old has been found still preserved.

  5. Yup & Nope

    Is the five-second rule real?

    Bacteria can transfer in under a second. But dry food picks up far less, and carpet passes on less than tile.

  6. Murmur

    One of these 8,300 dots is you

    Murmur is made of 8,300 dots, one for every million people alive. About six in ten live in Asia and nearly two in ten in Africa.

  7. Murmur

    Four people are born every second

    Every second about four people are born and two die. The world grows by roughly 190,000 people a day.

  8. Murmur

    You are part of the newest 7%

    About 117 billion people have ever been born. Everyone alive today is roughly 7% of the human story.
