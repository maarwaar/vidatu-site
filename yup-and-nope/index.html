Yup & Nope

Two halves of one creature.

The optimist half and the sceptic half argue one question with real facts. The meter always ends level and the audience votes in the comments. When they agree, they snap together into one face. Keep that rare.

Yup & Nope, live in your browser. Tap for an episode.

AlwaysEnd on the vote, never on a ruling.
NeverMake either one stupid; no politics or religion.

Episodes

  1. Round 001

    Could you outrun a hippo?

    Yup says easy. Nope has numbers: a hippo can run about 30 km/h on land, faster than most people can sprint.

  2. Round 002

    Is the five-second rule real?

    Bacteria can transfer in under a second. But dry food picks up far less, and carpet passes on less than tile.

  3. Round 003

    Would you move to Mars?

    About minus 60 degrees on average, air that is 95% carbon dioxide, and a day only 37 minutes longer than ours. But you would weigh about a third as much.
