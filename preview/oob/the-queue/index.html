Why do people queue when nobody makes them?

The short answer

Because everyone in the line accepts the same rule: first to arrive is first to be served. Usually nothing enforces it except the disapproval of the other people waiting.

Humans stand in a line, on purpose. There is often no fence, no guard and no written law. The first to arrive is served first, and everyone simply agrees.

If one human skips ahead, the others rarely do anything. They stare. Inspector Oob has recorded this stare as the strongest force observed so far, and has declined to test it personally.

This report is an observation, not a study. No numbers are claimed in it.
