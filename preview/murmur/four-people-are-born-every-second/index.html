Four people are born every second

The short answer

Every second about four people are born and two die. The world grows by roughly 190,000 people a day.

What we checked

About 4 births and 2 deaths per second; +69 million a year (about 190,000 a day); 363,000 births a day (one million in under 3 days).

Confirmed on 26 Nov 2026, against the source below.

Sources and notes

  • United Nations, World Population Prospects 2024. un.org
