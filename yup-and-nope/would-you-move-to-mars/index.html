Would you move to Mars?

The short answer

About minus 60 degrees on average, air that is 95% carbon dioxide, and a day only 37 minutes longer than ours. But you would weigh about a third as much.

What we checked

Mars gravity 38% (about a third), day 24 h 37 min, air 95% CO2 at under 1% of Earth's pressure.

Confirmed on 26 Nov 2026, against the source below.

Sources and notes

  • NASA/JPL, "Mars at a glance"; ESA, "Facts about Mars". jpl.nasa.gov
