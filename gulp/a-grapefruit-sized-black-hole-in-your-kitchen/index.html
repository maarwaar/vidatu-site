A grapefruit-sized black hole in your kitchen

The short answer

A black hole the size of a grapefruit would weigh about as much as seven Earths. Your kitchen would not survive the visit.

Sources and notes

  • NASA Imagine the Universe, "Black Holes": Earth squeezed to a 9 mm radius would be a black hole, so a 6 cm radius needs about seven Earths. imagine.gsfc.nasa.gov
