Tess

A being from the fourth dimension.

What we see is only her shadow. She performs "impossible" tricks that are simply geometry from one dimension up, and explains each with Sam, a flat square who lives on a sheet of paper.

Tess, live in your browser. Tap for an episode.

Episodes

  1. Trick 001

    This is not a cube

    Tess is four-dimensional, so what you see is only her shadow. A flat creature watching a ball pass through its world sees a dot, a circle, then nothing.

  2. Trick 002

    The impossible box trick (it is geometry)

    A ball leaves a sealed box with no lid and no door. A flat creature cannot leave a square, but you could lift it out.

  3. Trick 003

    How to turn a left shoe into a right shoe

    Slide and spin a flat letter F and it stays an F. Lift it off the page and flip it, and it becomes its mirror image.
