Gulp

A pocket-sized black hole.

Grapefruit-sized, polite, permanently hungry. Asks before eating and does not wait for the answer. Every episode has a real physics answer.

Episodes

  1. Snack 001

    What if a tiny black hole ate the Moon?

    Gulp is a polite, pocket-sized black hole. Without the Moon, tides would shrink to about a third, nights would be far darker, and Earth's tilt would slowly become unstable.

  2. Snack 002

    A grapefruit-sized black hole in your kitchen

    A black hole the size of a grapefruit would weigh about as much as seven Earths. Your kitchen would not survive the visit.

  3. Snack 003

    Can a black hole eat light?

    Light is the fastest thing there is, and even light cannot climb back out of a black hole. Light that only passes nearby gets bent.
